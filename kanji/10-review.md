# Kanji Daily Review

Five minutes a day. Every kanji and sign word you've practised is scheduled here with
**spaced repetition**: the better you know something, the longer until you see it again.
This deck is separate from your kana decks.

## How the schedule works

A correct answer moves an item up one box; a miss sends it back to box 1. Kanji are asked
both ways, meaning and reading, and the questions get harder as an item gets stronger.

| Box | Next review after | What you're asked |
|-----|-------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | 1 day | pick the meaning, find the kanji for a meaning, or pick the reading |
| 2 | 2 days | meaning, finding the kanji, typing the reading, or listening |
| 3–6 | 4, 8, 16, 32 days | mostly typing the reading, plus meaning and finding (box 5+ counts as mastered) |

**Tips**
- Review **before** you start a new unit.
- Back from a break with a lot due? Do 40 today and the rest tomorrow.
- "Extra practice" drills your 15 weakest items without changing the schedule.
