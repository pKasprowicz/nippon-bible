# Kanji 7: Toilets, Onsen and Hotels

The doors you really don't want to get wrong. Many toilets show pictograms, but onsen
curtains and older buildings often show only **男** and **女**, and the sides of an onsen
can swap from day to day.

**Your mission:** read **男湯 / 女湯**, **お手洗い**, **使用中 / 空き** and **温泉**.

## 2. Guess first

```quiz
title: Guess first
? 男 is a rice field (田) above strength (力). 女 is a kneeling figure. Which curtain do men go through?
- 男 *
- 女
> 男 (otoko) = man; 女 (onna) = woman. Check the kanji, not the colour.

? 湯 has water (氵) and the sun (日). On an onsen curtain it means…
- hot water, the bath *
- exit
- cold water
> 湯 (yu) = hot water. 男湯 = men's bath, 女湯 = women's bath.
```

## 5. Tell them apart

```contrast
湯 場 | Both end in 昜. 湯 has water (氵) on the left: hot water. 場 has earth (土): a place.
湯 温 | Both have water (氵). 湯's right side is a sun over rays; 温's is a sun over a dish (皿).
男 女 | 男 is a field (田) over strength (力); 女 is a kneeling figure.
```

## 9. Before you go

- **Onsen sides swap:** many onsen exchange the men's and women's baths daily so everyone gets both. Read **男湯 / 女湯** every time, even on your second visit.
- **空** also means "sky", and it's the first kanji of 空港 (*kūkō*, airport).
