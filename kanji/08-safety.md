# Kanji 8: Safety and Rules

Warnings, prohibitions, emergency exits, and the doors and lift buttons you meet every day.
Red signs with these kanji are worth stopping for.

**Your mission:** read **禁煙**, **禁止**, **止まれ**, **注意**, **危険**, **非常口**, and the
**押 / 引** and **開 / 閉** on doors and lifts.

## 2. Guess first

```quiz
title: Guess first
? 禁 means forbidden and 煙 means smoke (it has 火, fire, on the left). What's 禁煙?
- no smoking *
- smoking area
- fire alarm
> 禁煙 (kin'en) = no smoking.

? 開 and 閉 are both a gate (門). One has its bolt drawn back, one has a bar across. Which button opens the lift doors?
- 開 *
- 閉
> 開 (kai / hiraku) = open, 閉 (hei / shimaru) = close. Lift buttons usually show ◀▶ 開 and ▶◀ 閉.
```

## 6. Tell them apart

```contrast
開 閉 | Both are a gate (門). 開 has a drawn-back bolt (开) inside: open. 閉 has a bar (才) across: closed.
押 引 | 押 has a hand (扌): push. 引 has a bow (弓) with its string: pull.
止 上 | 止 has a short stroke on the left, like a footprint; 上 has only one arm, to the right.
```
