# Kanji 6: Opening Hours and Days

Is it open? Shop doors, restaurant windows and museum websites answer in kanji. The days of
the week are built from five elements plus the sun and moon, so seven kanji give you the
whole week.

**Your mission:** read **営業中** (open), **本日休業** (closed today), **定休日** (regular day
off) and all seven days.

## 2. Guess first

```quiz
title: Guess first
? 休 is a person (亻) leaning against a tree (木). On a shop door it means…
- closed, resting *
- open
- staff only
> 休 (kyū / yasumi) = rest. 本日休業 = closed today.

? Japanese days are named after the sun, the moon and five elements. 日 is the sun. Which day is 日曜日?
- Sunday *
- Monday
- Saturday
> 日曜日 (nichiyōbi) = sun-day, Sunday. 月曜日 = moon-day, Monday, just like in many European languages.
```

## 3. Meet the week

The days follow the order **sun, moon, fire, water, tree, gold, earth**. You already know
水 (water) and 金 (gold, money).

```cards
kana: 日月火木土曜
```

| Day | Kanji | Reading | Element |
|-----|-------|---------|---------|
| Monday | 月曜日 | *getsuyōbi* | moon |
| Tuesday | 火曜日 | *kayōbi* | fire |
| Wednesday | 水曜日 | *suiyōbi* | water |
| Thursday | 木曜日 | *mokuyōbi* | tree, wood |
| Friday | 金曜日 | *kin'yōbi* | gold, metal |
| Saturday | 土曜日 | *doyōbi* | earth |
| Sunday | 日曜日 | *nichiyōbi* | sun |

Signs and calendars often use only the first kanji in brackets: (月) = Monday, (土) = Saturday.

## 5. Tell them apart

```contrast
日 月 | 日 is a closed box with one bar inside; 月 is open at the bottom, with two bars.
木 本 | 本 is 木 with an extra mark across the bottom: the root.
土 上 | 土 has a long base and a short top bar; 上 has a vertical with one short arm to the right.
休 木 | 休 adds a person (亻) leaning on the tree.
```

Wednesdays. (時間 *jikan* = hours.)

## 9. Before you go

- **Museums** in Japan are often closed on 月曜日 (Monday). Check for 休館日 (*kyūkanbi*, closing day) on their websites.
- **日本** (*Nihon*) means "origin of the sun", which is why Japan is called the Land of the Rising Sun.
