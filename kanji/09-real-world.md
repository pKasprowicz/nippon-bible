# Kanji 9: Real-World Signs and Final Test

No new kanji: this unit mixes everything the way real signs do, then tests you on all 95.

**Your mission:** decode signs from a station, a restaurant, a shop, an onsen and the street,
then pass the final test.

## 9. Keep it alive

- **Daily review**, 5 minutes, for the next month: [open it](10-review.md).
- **Sign hunt in Japan:** every day, find five signs whose kanji you can read, and say them aloud.
- **When you meet an unknown kanji:** look for parts you know. 氵 means water is involved, 木 trees or wood, 口 a mouth or opening, 亻 a person, 扌 a hand. It's often enough to guess.
