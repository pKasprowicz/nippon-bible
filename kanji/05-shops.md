# Kanji 5: Shops and Money

Nine kanji for shopping: is the tax included, is there a discount, do they take cards?
Japanese price tags often show two prices, and these kanji tell you which is which.

**Your mission:** read **税込**, **税抜**, **割引**, **現金のみ** and **売り場**.

## 2. Guess first

```quiz
title: Guess first
? 税 means tax. 込 is "enter" (入) moving along a road: put in. So 税込 on a price tag means…
- tax included *
- tax free
- tax excluded
> 税込 (zeikomi) = tax included. 税抜 (zeinuki) = tax excluded: add about 10%.

? 金 is gold buried in the earth. 現 is "real, in your hand". What's 現金?
- cash *
- a gold shop
- a bank card
> 現金 (genkin) = cash. 現金のみ = cash only.
```

**割 counts tenths:** 1割引 = 10% off, 3割引 = 30% off. 半額 (*hangaku*) = half price.

## 9. Before you go

- **Two prices on one tag?** Since 2021 shops must show the tax-included price (税込), but many also show the price without tax (税抜), sometimes in bigger print. The 税込 price is what you pay.
- **金** also means Friday (金曜日), and **引** also means "pull" on doors (Unit 8).
