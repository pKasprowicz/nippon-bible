# Kanji 2: Entrances and Directions

The signs on every building and in every station: in, out, up, down, left, right and the
four compass points. Big stations name their exits by direction, so **北口** (north exit)
and **西口** (west exit) are how people arrange to meet.

**Your mission:** read **入口**, **出口**, **東口** and **北口**, and tell 左 from 右.

## 2. Guess first

```quiz
title: Guess first
? 上 and 下 are a line on the ground with a stroke pointing away from it. Which one means "up"?
- 上 *
- 下
> 上 (ue) points up; 下 (shita) hangs down. You'll see them on lift buttons and escalators.

? 口 means opening. 出 is two mountains stacked up: climb out. What's 出口?
- exit *
- entrance
- mountain path
> 出口 (deguchi) = exit. 入口 (iriguchi) = entrance.
```

## 6. Tell them apart

```contrast
左 右 | Both start with a hand (ナ). 左 has 工 (a tool) below it; 右 has 口 (a mouth).
上 下 | 上 points up from the ground line; 下 hangs down below it.
入 八 | 入: the right stroke is longer and starts from the left one. 八: two separate strokes spreading apart.
西 四 | 西 has a bar across the top and legs inside; 四 is a closed box with curtains.
```

## 10. Before you go

- **Meet at the right exit:** in a big station, "meet at the east exit" is *higashiguchi de*. Check which exit is closest to where you're going before you leave the platform.
- **東西** and **南北** are also Tokyo subway lines: the Tōzai line and the Namboku line. You'll read their full names in Unit 3.
