# Kanji 4: Restaurants and Food

Menus in tourist areas often have pictures or English, but small restaurants, meal-ticket
machines and supermarket labels don't. These twelve kanji tell you what's meat, what's fish,
what's a set meal and where the drinks are.

**Your mission:** read **定食**, **牛肉**, **豚肉**, **生ビール** and **食券**, and order from a ticket machine.

## 2. Guess first

```quiz
title: Guess first
? 肉 is a slab of marbled meat. 牛 is a cow. What do you get with 牛肉?
- beef *
- milk
- pork
> 牛肉 (gyūniku) = cow meat, beef. 豚肉 (butaniku) = pork.

? 魚 is a fish standing on its tail, with fins (灬) at the bottom. On a menu it means…
- fish *
- chicken
- fire-grilled
> 魚 (sakana) = fish.
```

## 5. Tell them apart

```contrast
牛 生 | 牛: one horn pokes up through the top bar. 生: a small extra stroke on the top left, nothing pokes through.
食 飲 | 飲 is 食 squeezed onto the left, with an open mouth (欠) on the right.
```

## 6. Read the menu

```words
title: Read the menu
定食 | teishoku | set meal | Menu heading
牛肉 | gyūniku | beef | Menu
豚肉 | butaniku | pork | Menu
魚 | sakana | fish | Menu
生ビール | nama bīru | draught beer | Izakaya menu
飲み物 | nomimono | drinks | Menu heading
食べ物 | tabemono | food | Menu heading
水 | mizu | water | Self-service counter
お茶 | ocha | green tea | Self-service counter
食券 | shokken | meal ticket | Machine at the entrance
```

**At a ticket machine (食券機):** find the button with 定食 or the dish you want, put money
in, press it, and hand the ticket (食券) to the staff. Buttons often show the price in
kanji or numerals: 八百円 = ¥800.
