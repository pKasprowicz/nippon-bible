# Kanji 3: Stations and Trains

Fourteen kanji that unlock every station: the platform number, the direction, the gates,
the ticket. Station signs usually add English, but timetables, ticket machines and
temporary notices often don't.

**Your mission:** read **駅**, **電車**, **2番線**, **方面**, **改札** and **切符**.

## 2. Guess first

```quiz
title: Guess first
? 電 is rain (雨) over a field with lightning: electricity. 車 is a cart from above. What's 電車?
- a train *
- a taxi
- a power station
> 電車 (densha) = electric vehicle, a train.

? 番 means number (in order) and 線 means line. On a platform sign, 3番線 is…
- platform 3 *
- line number 3 of a timetable
- the third car
> 番線 (bansen) = platform number. 3番線 = platform 3.
```

## 6. Tell them apart

```contrast
東 車 | 東 is the sun (日) behind a tree; 車 is a cart: 日 with an axle bar running through it.
方 万 | 方 has a dot on top; 万 doesn't.
上 下 | 上り (nobori) trains go towards the big city, 下り (kudari) trains go away from it.
```
