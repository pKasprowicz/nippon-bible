# 8. The Town

A street with the places a traveller looks for. The buildings carry picture signs only, so you learn to name them from their shapes and symbols.

**How to use the pictures.** In *Study*, tap anything to hear its name and see it written. *Find it* says a word and you tap the object; *Name it* circles an object and you choose its name. Words you miss go into your daily review in the [kana](../hiragana/00-start.md) and [kanji](../kanji/00-start.md) courses. Romaji is hidden until you switch it on.

### Asking for places

| Japanese | Kana | Romaji | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 近くにコンビニはありますか | ちかくにコンビニはありますか | *chikaku ni konbini wa arimasu ka* | Is there a convenience store near here? |
| 駅はどこですか | えきはどこですか | *eki wa doko desu ka* | Where is the station? |
| 歩いて何分ですか | あるいてなんぷんですか | *aruite nanpun desu ka* | How many minutes on foot? |
| 地図を書いてもらえますか | ちずをかいてもらえますか | *chizu o kaite moraemasu ka* | Could you draw me a map? |
