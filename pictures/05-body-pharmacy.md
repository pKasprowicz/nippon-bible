# 5. The Body and the Pharmacy

The parts of the body, the medicines on a pharmacy shelf, and how to say what hurts. This page teaches words, not medicine: ask a pharmacist or a doctor. For emergencies, see [Lesson 5](../lessons/05-situations.md).

**How to use the pictures.** In *Study*, tap anything to hear its name and see it written. *Find it* says a word and you tap the object; *Name it* circles an object and you choose its name. Words you miss go into your daily review in the [kana](../hiragana/00-start.md) and [kanji](../kanji/00-start.md) courses. Romaji is hidden until you switch it on.

### Saying what is wrong

Say the body part, then が痛いです (*ga itai desu*, "hurts"):

| Japanese | Kana | Romaji | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 頭が痛いです | あたまがいたいです | *atama ga itai desu* | I have a headache |
| お腹が痛いです | おなかがいたいです | *onaka ga itai desu* | I have a stomach ache |
| 喉が痛いです | のどがいたいです | *nodo ga itai desu* | I have a sore throat |
| 熱があります | ねつがあります | *netsu ga arimasu* | I have a fever |
| 咳が出ます | せきがでます | *seki ga demasu* | I have a cough |
| 気分が悪いです | きぶんがわるいです | *kibun ga warui desu* | I feel sick |

### At the pharmacy

| Japanese | Kana | Romaji | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 風邪薬はありますか | かぜぐすりはありますか | *kazegusuri wa arimasu ka* | Do you have cold medicine? |
| 一日何回ですか | いちにちなんかいですか | *ichinichi nankai desu ka* | How many times a day? |
| 食後に飲んでください | しょくごにのんでください | *shokugo ni nonde kudasai* | Take it after meals |
| 病院はどこですか | びょういんはどこですか | *byōin wa doko desu ka* | Where is a hospital? |

? What is 喉?

- お腹が痛いです

> 頭, atama, is the head.

? What is 目薬?
- eye drops *
- stomach medicine
- a face mask
> 目 is the eye, 薬 is medicine.
