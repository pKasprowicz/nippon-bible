# 3. Shops and Restaurants

Inside a convenience store and a restaurant: what you will see, and what the staff will say to you.

**How to use the pictures.** In *Study*, tap anything to hear its name and see it written. *Find it* says a word and you tap the object; *Name it* circles an object and you choose its name. Words you miss go into your daily review in the [kana](../hiragana/00-start.md) and [kanji](../kanji/00-start.md) courses. Romaji is hidden until you switch it on.

## Self-check

```quiz
? What is a 食券機?
- a machine where you buy a meal ticket *
- a cash machine
- a call button
> Buy the ticket first, then hand it to the staff ([Lesson 5](../lessons/05-situations.md)).

? The clerk asks 温めますか. What is the question?
- Shall I heat it up? *
- Do you need a bag?
- Is that everything?
> 温める, atatameru, means to warm up.

? What is お冷?
- a glass of water *
- the bill
- a hand towel
> お (o-) + 冷 (cold): cold water.
```
