# 2. Food

A set meal on a tray, a bowl of ramen, a sushi counter, and the words for how food tastes. For how Japanese food is made, see the [Cuisine handbook](../cuisine/00-start.md); for ordering, [Lesson 9](../lessons/09-food.md).

**How to use the pictures.** In *Study*, tap anything to hear its name and see it written. *Find it* says a word and you tap the object; *Name it* circles an object and you choose its name. Words you miss go into your daily review in the [kana](../hiragana/00-start.md) and [kanji](../kanji/00-start.md) courses. Romaji is hidden until you switch it on.

## 2.1 A set meal

A 定食 (*teishoku*) is a set meal: rice, soup, a main dish and small sides on one tray.


### At the table

| Japanese | Kana | Romaji | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| いただきます | いただきます | *itadakimasu* | said before eating |
| ごちそうさまでした | ごちそうさまでした | *gochisōsama deshita* | said after eating: thank you for the meal |

## Self-check

```quiz
? What is 箸?
- chopsticks *
- a chopstick rest
- a rice bowl
> The rest is 箸置き, hashioki.

? Which word means "spicy"?
- 辛い (karai) *
- 甘い (amai)
- 苦い (nigai)
> 甘い is sweet, 苦い bitter.

? What is ガリ at a sushi counter?
- pickled ginger *
- wasabi
- soy sauce
> Sliced ginger in sweet vinegar.
```
