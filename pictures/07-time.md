# 7. Time and the Week

Telling the time, the parts of the day, and the days of the week. Each weekday's first kanji is a picture: the moon, fire, water, a tree, gold, earth and the sun.

**How to use the pictures.** In *Study*, tap anything to hear its name and see it written. *Find it* says a word and you tap the object; *Name it* circles an object and you choose its name. Words you miss go into your daily review in the [kana](../hiragana/00-start.md) and [kanji](../kanji/00-start.md) courses. Romaji is hidden until you switch it on.

## 7.1 Telling the time

The hour is the number + 時 (*ji*); minutes are 分 (*fun* or *pun*), and half past is 半 (*han*). Note the irregular 四時 (*yoji*), 七時 (*shichiji*) and 九時 (*kuji*).

### Asking about time

| Japanese | Kana | Romaji | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 今何時ですか | いまなんじですか | *ima nanji desu ka* | What time is it now? |
| 何時に開きますか | なんじにあきますか | *nanji ni akimasu ka* | What time does it open? |
| 何時までですか | なんじまでですか | *nanji made desu ka* | Until what time (is it open)? |
| 今日は何曜日ですか | きょうはなんようびですか | *kyō wa nan'yōbi desu ka* | What day is it today? |
