# Picture Dictionary: Words You Can See

Words stick when you can see them. This module teaches about 220 everyday words and 70 phrases with drawn pictures: point at a thing, hear its name, see how it is written, and test yourself.

**How it works**

- **Study.** Tap anything in a picture, or its label, to hear the word and see it in kanji, kana and English. The word is highlighted in the table under the picture.
- **Find it.** You hear and see a word; tap the right object.
- **Name it.** An object is circled; choose its name from four.
- **Review.** Words you miss in a quiz go into your daily review in the [hiragana](../hiragana/00-start.md), [katakana](../katakana/00-start.md) or [kanji](../kanji/00-start.md) course, by how the word is written. *Add all to my review* adds a whole picture at once.
- **Romaji** is hidden at first, so you read the kana. Switch it on above any picture.

Labels show the word as it is usually written on signs and menus, with kanji where Japanese uses kanji, and its kana reading underneath.
