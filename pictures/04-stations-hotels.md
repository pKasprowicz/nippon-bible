# 4. Stations and Hotels

A station from the ticket machines to the platform, and a hotel lobby. For how fares and tickets work, see the [Railways handbook](../railways/09-tickets.md); for the words on station signs, [Railway Japanese](../railways/13-language.md).

**How to use the pictures.** In *Study*, tap anything to hear its name and see it written. *Find it* says a word and you tap the object; *Name it* circles an object and you choose its name. Words you miss go into your daily review in the [kana](../hiragana/00-start.md) and [kanji](../kanji/00-start.md) courses. Romaji is hidden until you switch it on.

### At the station

| Japanese | Kana | Romaji | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 〜までいくらですか | 〜までいくらですか | *... made ikura desu ka* | How much is it to ...? |
| この電車は〜に行きますか | このでんしゃは〜にいきますか | *kono densha wa ... ni ikimasu ka* | Does this train go to ...? |
| 何番線ですか | なんばんせんですか | *nanbansen desu ka* | Which platform? |
| 次の電車は何時ですか | つぎのでんしゃはなんじですか | *tsugi no densha wa nanji desu ka* | When is the next train? |
