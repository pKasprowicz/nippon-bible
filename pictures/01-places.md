# 1. Where Things Are and Finding the Way

Twelve small pictures show one box (or a table) and a red ball, each time in a different place. Then an intersection seen from above teaches the words for asking and giving directions.

**How to use the pictures.** In *Study*, tap anything to hear its name and see it written. *Find it* says a word and you tap the object; *Name it* circles an object and you choose its name. Words you miss go into your daily review in the [kana](../hiragana/00-start.md) and [kanji](../kanji/00-start.md) courses. Romaji is hidden until you switch it on.

### How the words are used

Japanese puts the place word **after** the thing it is measured from, joined by の (*no*):

| Japanese | Kana | Romaji | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 箱の上 | はこのうえ | *hako no ue* | on (top of) the box |
| 机の下 | つくえのした | *tsukue no shita* | under the table |
| 箱の中に | はこのなかに | *hako no naka ni* | in the box |
| 駅の前で | えきのまえで | *eki no mae de* | in front of the station |
| 銀行の隣 | ぎんこうのとなり | *ginkō no tonari* | next to the bank |
| ここ · そこ · あそこ | ここ · そこ · あそこ | *koko · soko · asoko* | here · there (near you) · over there |

### Asking the way

| Japanese | Kana | Romaji | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 〜はどこですか | 〜はどこですか | *... wa doko desu ka* | Where is ...? |
| トイレはどこですか | トイレはどこですか | *toire wa doko desu ka* | Where is the toilet? |
| まっすぐ行ってください | まっすぐいってください | *massugu itte kudasai* | Please go straight on |
| 右に曲がってください | みぎにまがってください | *migi ni magatte kudasai* | Please turn right |
| 信号を左です | しんごうをひだりです | *shingō o hidari desu* | Left at the traffic lights |
| 近いですか | ちかいですか | *chikai desu ka* | Is it near? |
| 遠いですか | とおいですか | *tōi desu ka* | Is it far? |

## Self-check

```quiz
? What does 上 (ue) mean?
- on, above *
- under
- behind
> Its opposite is 下, shita.

? How do you say "in front of the station"?
- 駅の前 (eki no mae) *
- 前の駅 (mae no eki)
- 駅の後ろ (eki no ushiro)
> The place word comes after の.

? What is 交差点?
- an intersection *
- a traffic light
- a pedestrian crossing
> 信号 is the traffic light, 横断歩道 the crossing.
```
