# 9. Rooms and the Bath

A Japanese-style room, as in a *ryokan*, and a toilet and bathroom. For bathing customs, see the [Bathing handbook](../bathing/00-start.md).

**How to use the pictures.** In *Study*, tap anything to hear its name and see it written. *Find it* says a word and you tap the object; *Name it* circles an object and you choose its name. Words you miss go into your daily review in the [kana](../hiragana/00-start.md) and [kanji](../kanji/00-start.md) courses. Romaji is hidden until you switch it on.

## 9.1 A Japanese-style room

A 和室 (*washitsu*) is a room with *tatami* floors. Take your slippers off before you step on the *tatami* ([Lesson 7](../lessons/07-etiquette.md)).

```picture
washitsu
```

## 9.2 The toilet and the bath

Toilet flushes are marked 大 (big) and 小 (small) for a full or a half flush, and you wash on the stool before you get into the bath ([Lesson 7](../lessons/07-etiquette.md)).

### Useful phrases

| Japanese | Kana | Romaji | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| お風呂は何時までですか | おふろはなんじまでですか | *o-furo wa nanji made desu ka* | Until what time is the bath open? |
| タオルはありますか | タオルはありますか | *taoru wa arimasu ka* | Are there towels? |
| お湯が出ません | おゆがでません | *o-yu ga demasen* | There is no hot water |
| トイレが流れません | トイレがながれません | *toire ga nagaremasen* | The toilet will not flush |

## Self-check

```quiz
? What is 布団?
- the bedding laid on the floor *
- a floor cushion
- a low table
> The cushion is 座布団, zabuton.

? On a flush lever, what does 小 mean?
- small (half) flush *
- big (full) flush
- hot water
> 大 is the full flush (Lesson 7).

? What is 湯船?
- the bathtub *
- the shower
- the washbowl
> 湯 is hot water, 船 a boat.
```
