# Glossary

Japanese and technical terms used in this handbook. The chapter where each is explained is given in brackets, and the explanation there carries the source.

## Japanese terms

| Term | Meaning | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| ***[aburaage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abura-age)*** | Thin slices of [tofu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tofu) fried in oil, with the surface dried | 11 |
| ***amakuchi*** ([miso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miso)) | Mildly salty miso | 4 |
| ***ama-miso*** | Sweet miso: high [kōji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C5%8Dji_%28food%29), low salt | 4 |
| ***[an](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_bean_paste)*** | Sweet paste of cooked beans, made industrially in Japan mainly from red [adzuki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adzuki_bean) beans | 12 |
| ***an-ryūshi*** | "An-particles": the intact bean cells that make up an | 12 |
| ***arabushi*** | [Katsuobushi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katsuobushi) after simmering and smoking, before moulding | 2 |
| ***baikan*** | Smoke-drying of katsuobushi | 2 |
| ***chiai*** | Dark muscle of bonito, removed for the finest [dashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dashi) | 2 |
| ***[daifuku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daifuku)*** | [Mochi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mochi) made from [glutinous rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glutinous_rice) flour, wrapped around a filling | 13 |
| ***dashi*** | Japanese stock extracted from [kombu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kombu), katsuobushi, [shiitake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiitake) and others | 2 |
| ***futa*** | Wooden tray used in hand-made kōji | 3 |
| ***giongo*** / ***gitaigo*** | [Onomatopoeia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_sound_symbolism) that imitate real sounds / depict non-sound impressions | 14 |
| ***hazekomi*** | Penetration of kōji hyphae into the rice grain | 3 |
| ***hikikomi*** | Loading inoculated rice into the kōji room | 3 |
| ***hishio*** | Early salt-fermented foods, ancestor of [soy sauce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soy_sauce) | 4 |
| ***honjōzō*** | The standard Japanese soy sauce brewing method | 4 |
| ***honkarebushi*** | The highest grade of fully moulded katsuobushi | 2 |
| ***ichiban dashi*** / ***niban dashi*** | First stock / second stock from the spent ingredients | 2 |
| ***[ikejime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ikejime)*** | Killing a fish by spiking the brain and destroying the spinal cord with a wire | 6 |
| ***kabitsuke*** | Moulding stage of katsuobushi | 2 |
| ***[kanten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agar)*** | Agar | 12 |
| ***karakuchi*** (miso) | Salty miso | 4 |
| ***karebushi*** | Katsuobushi that has been moulded | 2 |
| ***kasu*** vinegar | Vinegar made from [sake lees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sake_kasu) | 5 |
| ***katsuobushi*** | [Skipjack tuna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skipjack_tuna) simmered, smoked and moulded until rock-hard | 2 |
| ***kinugoshi-dōfu*** | Fine, soft tofu, set without pressing | 11 |
| ***kirikaeshi*** | Hand-kneading of kōji to release heat | 3 |
| ***kōji*** | Grain or beans grown over with *[Aspergillus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspergillus)* mould; also the mould itself | 3 |
| ***koikuchi*** | Dark soy sauce, at least 80% of production | 4 |
| ***kombu*** | Dried kelp | 2 |
| ***kōri-dōfu*** (*[kōya-dōfu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koya-dofu)*) | [Freeze-dried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freeze_drying) tofu | 11 |
| ***koshi-an*** | Smooth sweetened an | 12 |
| ***kuragakoi*** | Cellar maturing of kombu | 2 |
| ***kurozu*** | Amber [rice vinegar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_vinegar) from long spontaneous fermentation in jars | 5 |
| ***[kuzu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kudzu)*** | Kudzu (*Pueraria*) [starch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starch) | 13 |
| ***marunuki*** | [Buckwheat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckwheat) flour ground from the whole de-hulled grain | 10 |
| ***[mirin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirin)*** | Sweet seasoning made from glutinous rice, kōji and [shōchū](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Dch%C5%AB) | 5 |
| ***mochi*** | Cake of glutinous rice | 13 |
| ***mochi-mochi*** | Soft, elastic, chewy texture | 10, 14 |
| ***momen-dōfu*** | Coarse, firm tofu, pressed | 11 |
| ***moromi*** | Fermenting mash of kōji and brine (soy sauce) or kōji and alcohol (mirin) | 4, 5 |
| ***moyashi*** | Spore starter for [sake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sake) brewers | 3 |
| ***namaribushi*** | Bonito after simmering, before smoking | 2 |
| ***[niboshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niboshi)*** | Small dried sardines or anchovies | 2 |
| ***[nigari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bittern_%28salt%29)*** | Bittern, the traditional tofu coagulant | 11 |
| ***nikuzure*** | Simmered food falling apart, which mirin helps prevent | 5 |
| ***[okara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okara_%28food%29)*** | [Soybean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soybean) solids removed when making [soy milk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soy_milk) | 11 |
| ***orisage*** | Sedimentation step in mirin making | 5 |
| ***saishikomi*** | "Re-brewed" soy sauce, made with soy sauce instead of brine | 4 |
| ***sarashi-an*** (*nama-an*) | Unsweetened, washed bean paste | 12 |
| ***sarashina*** | Flour from the inner part of the buckwheat grain; pale [soba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soba) | 10 |
| ***shajuku*** | Simmering of bonito fillets | 2 |
| ***shibukiri*** | Discarding the first cooking water of adzuki beans | 12 |
| ***shikomi*** | Mixing the ingredients of miso | 4 |
| ***shio-kōji*** | Kōji fermented with salt and water: a seasoning with active enzymes | 3, 8 |
| ***shiro*** (soy sauce) | White soy sauce, made mostly from wheat | 4 |
| ***shōjin dashi*** | Vegetarian temple stock | 2 |

| ***[yuba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tofu_skin)*** | Film formed on heated soy milk | 11 |

## Technical terms

| Term | Meaning | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| **[Amylase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amylase)** | Enzyme that breaks starch into sugars | 3 |
| **[Amylopectin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amylopectin)** | The highly branched starch molecule | 9, 13 |
| **[Amylose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amylose)** | The mostly linear starch molecule | 9 |
| **Apparent amylose content (AAC)** | Amylose measured by the [iodine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iodine%E2%80%93starch_test) method | 9 |
| **[Gelatinisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starch_gelatinization)** | Conversion of raw (β) starch into cooked (α) starch by heat and water | 3, 9 |
| **Gelation [hysteresis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hysteresis)** | A gel that sets at a much lower temperature than it melts at, as agar does | 12 |
| **GMP** (5′-guanylate) | [Umami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umami) [nucleotide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nucleotide), from shiitake | 1 |
| **IMP** (5′-inosinate) | Umami nucleotide, from fish and meat | 1 |
| **Ion bridge** | Divalent ions such as Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺ linking protein chains | 11 |
| **K value** | Freshness index based on how far [ATP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adenosine_triphosphate) has broken down in fish | 6 |
| **Leachate** | Material that leaks out of rice grains during cooking | 9 |
| **[Maillard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maillard_reaction) reaction** | Browning reaction between sugars and amino compounds | 4, 7 |
| **Parallel double fermentation** | Saccharification and alcohol fermentation in one vessel at once | 3, 5 |
| **[Phytic acid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phytic_acid)** | Phosphate compound in seeds that binds calcium | 11 |
| **[Protease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protease)** | Enzyme that breaks down protein | 3, 8 |
| **[Retrogradation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retrogradation_%28starch%29)** | Re-ordering of cooked starch on cooling and storage: staling | 9, 13 |
| **[Rigor mortis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rigor_mortis)** | Stiffening of muscle after death as ATP runs out | 6 |
| **[Syneresis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syneresis_%28chemistry%29)** | Weeping of liquid from a gel | 12 |
| **Synergy** (umami) | The multiplied umami of [glutamate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glutamic_acid) plus a nucleotide | 1 |
