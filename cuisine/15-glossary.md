# Glossary

Japanese and technical terms used in this handbook. The chapter where each is explained is given in brackets, and the explanation there carries the source.

## Technical terms

| Term | Meaning | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| **Amylase** | Enzyme that breaks starch into sugars | 3 |
| **Amylopectin** | The highly branched starch molecule | 9, 13 |
| **Amylose** | The mostly linear starch molecule | 9 |
| **Apparent amylose content (AAC)** | Amylose measured by the iodine method | 9 |
| **Gelatinisation** | Conversion of raw (β) starch into cooked (α) starch by heat and water | 3, 9 |
| **Gelation hysteresis** | A gel that sets at a much lower temperature than it melts at, as agar does | 12 |
| **GMP** (5′-guanylate) | Umami nucleotide, from shiitake | 1 |
| **IMP** (5′-inosinate) | Umami nucleotide, from fish and meat | 1 |
| **Ion bridge** | Divalent ions such as Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺ linking protein chains | 11 |
| **K value** | Freshness index based on how far ATP has broken down in fish | 6 |
| **Leachate** | Material that leaks out of rice grains during cooking | 9 |
| **Maillard reaction** | Browning reaction between sugars and amino compounds | 4, 7 |
| **Parallel double fermentation** | Saccharification and alcohol fermentation in one vessel at once | 3, 5 |
| **Phytic acid** | Phosphate compound in seeds that binds calcium | 11 |
| **Protease** | Enzyme that breaks down protein | 3, 8 |
| **Retrogradation** | Re-ordering of cooked starch on cooling and storage: staling | 9, 13 |
| **Rigor mortis** | Stiffening of muscle after death as ATP runs out | 6 |
| **Syneresis** | Weeping of liquid from a gel | 12 |
| **Synergy** (umami) | The multiplied umami of glutamate plus a nucleotide | 1 |
