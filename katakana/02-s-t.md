# Katakana 2: The サ and タ Rows

Ten new characters, including the most famous look-alike pair in Japanese: **シ** (shi)
and **ツ** (tsu). Two sounds are irregular, as in hiragana: **シ = shi**, **チ = chi**,
**ツ = tsu**.

**Your mission:** read **タクシー** (taxi), **スーツケース** (suitcase) and
**ステーキ** (steak), and learn how English words become katakana.

## 2. Guess first

```quiz
title: Guess first
? シ and ツ look alike. In シ, the dashes are stacked on the left and the long stroke sweeps UP. Which one is "shi"?
- シ *
- ツ
> シ = shi: dashes on the left, stroke sweeping up, like a smile. ツ = tsu: dashes along the top, stroke falling down, like a wave.

? タ looks like ク with an extra dash inside. What might it be?
- ta *
- ku
- ke
> タ = ta: ク "ta"king something inside.
```

## 5. Tell them apart

Take your time here: these pairs cause most katakana reading mistakes.

```contrast
シ ツ | シ: dashes on the LEFT, stroke sweeps UP. ツ: dashes along the TOP, stroke falls DOWN.
ク タ | タ is ク with an extra dash inside.
チ テ | チ has a slash on top; テ has a short flat bar.
サ セ | サ: two posts cross the bar. セ: one post with a hook, curving right.
ケ タ | ケ's long stroke drops straight down from the bar; タ is a closed ク with a dash.
```

## 6. How English becomes katakana

Japanese syllables almost always end in a vowel, so borrowed words gain vowels. Learn
these patterns and you can decode most loanwords:

| Pattern | Example |
|---------|---------|
| A **t** or **d** with no vowel gets **o** | tes**t** → テス**ト** *tesu-to* |
| Other consonants with no vowel get **u** | **s**ki → **ス**キー *su-kī* |
| English long vowels and **-er / -or / -ar** become the long bar **ー** | heat**er** → ヒータ**ー** *hī-tā* |
| **ti** and **di** become **chi** and **ji** in older words | ti**ck**et → チケット |
| **r** and **l** are both the ラ row | hote**l** → ホテ**ル**, **r**estaurant → **レ**ストラン |

```quiz
title: Decode it
? テスト (tesuto) comes from which English word?
- taste
- test *
- toast
> tes-t + o: the final t gets an o. "Toast" is トースト (tōsuto).

? Which katakana is "ski"?
- スキー *
- シキ
- スカイ
> s gets a u: su-kī, and the long "ee" becomes ー.

? スーツケース: what is it?
- suitcase *
- sweets case
- suit and case
> sū-tsu-kē-su: suit (スーツ) + case (ケース).
```

## 13. Before you go

- **シ or ツ?** Draw both from memory on paper. Which way does the long stroke go? (シ sweeps up, ツ falls down.)
- **Spot it:** taxi ranks are signed **タクシー** (often with のりば, boarding place), and the red sign in a taxi's windscreen, 空車, means it's available.
