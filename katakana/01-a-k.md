# Katakana 1: The ア and カ Rows, and ー

Ten characters and one very useful sign. The vowels always come in the same order,
**a, i, u, e, o**, so the カ row is **ka ki ku ke ko**. Several of them look like their
hiragana twins: **カ/か**, **キ/き**, and **ウ/う** with a roof.

**The long bar ー** makes the vowel before it twice as long: **ケーキ** is *kē-ki*
(cake). In vertical text it's drawn vertically, as **｜**.

**Your mission:** read **ケーキ** (cake), **ココア** (cocoa) and **オーケー** (OK).

## 1. Guess first

```quiz
title: Guess first
? カ looks almost exactly like a hiragana character you may know. Which sound is it?
- ka *
- ki
- ko
> カ is hiragana か without the little dash: ka.

? コ is two strokes forming the corner of a box. Its sound is…
- ko *
- ku
- ke
> コ = ko, the "co"rner of a box.
```

## 4. Tell them apart

```contrast
ク ケ | ク is a roof with one leg. ケ's top bar is separate and its long stroke drops straight down from it.
エ コ | エ is an I-beam: bars top and bottom joined by a post. コ is an open box corner.
ア オ | ア has a hooked top bar and a curved tail. オ is a cross with a kick.
ウ ク | ウ has a dash on top and a box-lid. ク has no dash and only one leg.
```

These are words borrowed from English. Sound each one out, using ー to stretch the vowel,
then pick its meaning.
