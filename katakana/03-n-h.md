# Katakana 3: The ナ and ハ Rows

Ten more. Some are gifts: **ニ** is the kanji 二 ("two", read *ni*), **ヘ** is identical to
hiragana へ, and **ハ** looks like 八 ("eight", *hachi*), a mouth laughing "ha ha". The
irregular sound is **フ = fu**, a soft "f" blown between the lips.

**Your mission:** read **コーヒー** (coffee), **ナイフ** (knife) and **スニーカー**
(sneakers).

## 2. Guess first

```quiz
title: Guess first
? ニ is two lines, exactly like the kanji for "two". How is "two" read in Japanese?
- ni *
- na
- no
> 二 is "ni", and so is ニ.

? ヘ looks exactly like a hiragana character. Which sound do you think it is?
- he *
- ha
- ho
> ヘ and へ are the same little hill: he.
```

## 5. Tell them apart

```contrast
ヌ ス | ヌ: the slash crosses the フ shape. ス: the leg kicks out below, nothing crosses.
ホ オ | ホ is a cross with two small feet, like 木. オ is a cross with one kick to the left.
ヒ セ | ヒ is a bar and a hook. セ has a post crossing a hooked bar.
ニ コ | ニ is two separate lines. コ joins them into a corner.
ノ ソ | ノ is a single slash. ソ adds a short dash on the left.
ハ ヘ | ハ is two strokes spreading apart. ヘ is one stroke making a hill.
```
