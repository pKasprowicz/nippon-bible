# Katakana 7: Small ッ, Combinations and Extra Sounds

Three spelling tricks, and you can read any katakana word:

1. **Small ッ** = a short pause that doubles the next consonant: **チケット** *chiketto* (ticket)
2. **Small ャ ュ ョ** merge with the character before, as in hiragana: **メニュー** *menyū* (menu)
3. **Small ァ ィ ゥ ェ ォ** make sounds Japanese doesn't have, for foreign words:
   **パーティー** *pātī* (party), **カフェ** *kafe* (café), **フォーク** *fōku* (fork)

**Your mission:** read **チケット**, **メニュー**, **ジュース**, **シャワー**,
**チェックイン** and **ファミリーマート**.

## 2. Small ッ: the pause

**ッ** isn't pronounced. It's a one-beat silence, so the next consonant doubles in romaji.
English words with a short vowel before a hard consonant often get one:

| Word | Reading | From |
|------|---------|------|
| チケット | chi**k**e**tt**o | ticket |
| ベッド | be**dd**o | bed |
| ロッカー | ro**kk**ā | locker |
| ホット | ho**tt**o | hot (drinks) |
| ネット | ne**tt**o | (inter)net |

## 3. Small ャ ュ ョ

The same table as in hiragana: **キャ kya, シュ shu, チョ cho, ジャ ja, ニュ nyu**… Drop
the *i*, add *ya / yu / yo*.

## 4. Extra sounds for foreign words

Japanese has no "ti", "di", "fa" or "she". Katakana builds them with a **small vowel**:

| Sound | Built from | Example |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| **ティ** ti | テ + small ィ | パーティー · *pātī* · party |
| **ディ** di | デ + small ィ | ディズニー · *dizunī* · Disney |
| **ファ フィ フェ フォ** fa fi fe fo | フ + small vowel | ファミリー · *famirī* · family, カフェ · café, フォーク · fork |
| **ウィ ウェ** wi we | ウ + small vowel | ウェブ · *webu* · web |
| **シェ ジェ チェ** she je che | シ/ジ/チ + small ェ | シェフ · chef, チェック · check |

```quiz
title: Use the rule
? How do you read ティ?
- ti *
- chi
- teyi
> テ (te) + small ィ = "ti", one beat. チ is "chi". Older loanwords use チ instead: チケット (ticket).

? What's the difference between ファ and フア?
- none
- ファ is one beat, "fa"; フア is two beats, "fu-a" *
> Small vowels merge; big ones are a separate beat.

? カフェ is a…
- café *
- coffee
- cafeteria
> ka-fe: café. Coffee is コーヒー.
```

## 5. Tell them apart

```contrast
ティ チ | ティ is "ti" (テ + small ィ); チ is "chi".
ディ ジ | ディ is "di"; ジ is "ji".
シェ セ | シェ is "she"; セ is "se".
キャ キョ | Small ャ = ya; small ョ = yo.
シュ ジュ | No mark = shu; two dashes = ju.
ファ フォ | Small ァ = fa; small ォ = fo.
```

## 11. Before you go

- You can now read **any katakana word**. The last unit takes you to real menus, shops and signs.
- **Small or big?** If a ャ, ッ or ィ looks smaller than its neighbours, it merges or pauses. If it's full size, it's its own beat.
