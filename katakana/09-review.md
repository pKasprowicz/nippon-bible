# Katakana Daily Review

Five minutes a day. Every katakana character and word you've practised is scheduled here
with **spaced repetition**: the better you know something, the longer until you see it
again. This deck is separate from your hiragana deck.

```review
```

## How the schedule works

A correct answer moves an item up one box; a miss sends it back to box 1.

| Box | Next review after | What you're asked |
|-----|-------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | 1 day | recognise it (read, or pick what you hear) |
| 2 | 2 days | recognise it or type it |
| 3–6 | 4, 8, 16, 32 days | type the reading, or pick what you hear (box 5+ counts as mastered) |

**Tips**
- Review **before** you start a new unit.
- Back from a break with a lot due? Do 40 today and the rest tomorrow.
- "Extra practice" drills your 15 weakest items without changing the schedule.

## Free play

An endless game with **every katakana in your review deck**, asked checkpoint-style,
weakest first. It doesn't change your review schedule.

```game
title: Free play: everything you've learned
pool: learned
modes: type,listen,type
```
