# Katakana 6: ゛ and ゜ (Voiced Sounds)

The same rule as in hiragana: **゛** voices a sound (**k→g, s→z, t→d, h→b**) and **゜**
turns the ハ row into **p**. Learn the rule once and you get 25 more sounds, plus the
**b**, **v**, **g** and **p** of countless foreign words.

**Your mission:** read **コンビニ**, **ビール**, **デパート**, **パスポート**, and the
katakana names of several European countries.

## 2. Work out the rule

```quiz
title: Work it out
? カ is "ka". What's ガ (カ + ゛)?
- ga *
- ka
- ha
> ゛ voices the consonant: k → g.

? English "v" has no Japanese equivalent. Which row do you think it uses, as in サービス (service)?
- the バ row (b) *
- the ワ row (w)
- the フ row (f)
> v becomes b: ビデオ (video), サービス (service). A rare ヴ also exists, but you'll seldom need it.

? ハ is "ha". What's パ?
- pa *
- ba
- fa
> ゜ always makes p: パ ピ プ ペ ポ.
```

## 3. The whole table

| Base row | + ゛ | + ゜ |
|----------|------|------|
| カ ka キ ki ク ku ケ ke コ ko | **ガ ga ギ gi グ gu ゲ ge ゴ go** | |
| サ sa シ shi ス su セ se ソ so | **ザ za ジ ji ズ zu ゼ ze ゾ zo** | |
| タ ta チ chi ツ tsu テ te ト to | **ダ da ヂ ji ヅ zu デ de ド do** | |
| ハ ha ヒ hi フ fu ヘ he ホ ho | **バ ba ビ bi ブ bu ベ be ボ bo** | **パ pa ピ pi プ pu ペ pe ポ po** |

**ヂ** and **ヅ** are very rare: use **ジ** and **ズ** for *ji* and *zu*.

## 5. Tell them apart

```contrast
バ パ | Two dashes = b. Small circle = p.
ビ ピ | Two dashes = b. Small circle = p.
ブ プ | Two dashes = b. Small circle = p.
シ ジ | No mark = shi. Two dashes = ji.
ツ ズ | ツ (tsu) and ズ (zu) are different bases: ズ is ス + ゛.
```

## 7. Europe in katakana

Country names often come from the language Japan first met them in, not from English:
**ドイツ** from Dutch *Duits*, **オランダ** from Portuguese *Holanda*.

## 13. Before you go

- **Tell Japanese people where you're from:** *[country] kara kimashita* (I came from …). Try it with your country's katakana name.
- **サービス** in a shop or restaurant often means "free, on the house". A pleasant surprise to be able to read!
