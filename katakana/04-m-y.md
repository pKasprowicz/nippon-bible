# Katakana 4: The マ and ヤ Rows

Eight characters. More gifts: **ミ** looks like 三 ("three", as in *mittsu*), **モ** and
**ヤ** look like their hiragana twins も and や. Watch out for **ユ** (yu) and **コ** (ko).

**Your mission:** read **ホーム** (station platform), **マスク** (mask), **ハム** (ham)
and **トマト**.

## 2. Guess first

```quiz
title: Guess first
? ミ is three slanted lines, like 三 (three). In "mittsu" (three things), which sound starts the word?
- mi *
- ma
- mu
> ミ = mi.

? ユ and コ look alike. ユ's bottom bar sticks out past the corner. Which one is "yu"?
- ユ *
- コ
> ユ = yu: like a U lying on its side, with an overhang. コ = ko: a neat corner.
```

## 5. Tell them apart

```contrast
マ ム | マ is a bar bending to a point with a dash. ム is a triangle with a tail.
ユ コ | ユ's bottom bar sticks out; コ's doesn't.
ヨ ユ | ヨ has three bars; ユ has two.
ミ シ | ミ is three slanted lines; シ is two dashes and a long stroke sweeping up.
メ ナ | メ is a slanted X; ナ is an upright plus with a curved leg.
ヤ セ | ヤ: a hooked bar with a slash. セ: a hooked bar crossed by a post that curves right.
モ キ | モ's post curves right at the bottom; キ's goes straight through.
```
