# Katakana 5: The ラ and ワ Rows, and ン

The last eight basic characters, and with them the words a traveller reads most:
**ホテル**, **トイレ**, **レストラン**. Remember that Japanese has one sound for both
English **r** and **l**, so both become the ラ row.

**Your mission:** read **ホテル**, **トイレ**, **レストラン**, **ラーメン** and
**エスカレーター**. After this unit you know **all 46 basic katakana**.

## 2. Guess first

```quiz
title: Guess first
? ロ is a closed box, like the kanji 口 (mouth). Which sound is it?
- ro *
- ko
- ra
> ロ = ro. コ (ko) is open on the left; ロ is closed.

? ン and ソ are a famous look-alike pair. In ン, the long stroke sweeps UP from the bottom. Which one is "n"?
- ン *
- ソ
> ン = n (stroke sweeps up, like シ). ソ = so (strokes fall down, like ツ).
```

## 5. Tell them apart

The two families to watch: **シ ツ ソ ン** (dashes and one long stroke) and **ル レ**.

```contrast
ソ ン | ソ: both strokes fall DOWN from the top. ン: the long stroke sweeps UP from the bottom.
シ ン | シ has two dashes; ン has one.
ツ ソ | ツ has three strokes; ソ has two.
ル レ | ル has two legs; レ is the right leg alone.
ワ ウ | ウ has a dash on top; ワ doesn't.
ラ フ | ラ has an extra short bar above the フ shape.
ロ コ | ロ is a closed box; コ is open on the left.
リ ソ | リ is two upright strokes, the right one long. ソ's strokes slant towards each other.
```

## 8. Made-in-Japan English

Some katakana words *look* English but mean something else (*wasei-eigo*,
Japan-made English). They're worth knowing before you travel:

```words
title: Japan-made English
mode: meaning
コンセント | konsento | power socket (not "consent")
サラリーマン | sararīman | office worker
ノルマ | noruma | quota, target (from Russian "norma")
クーラー | kūrā | air conditioner
```
