# 2. Voiced Sounds: ゛ and ゜

Characters with the two small marks: が to ぽ and ガ to ポ. Every test mixes **hiragana and katakana**. There is no teaching here: each answer is marked at once, and at the end a report shows every character you met, coloured by how you did, with your weakest characters and a link to the unit that teaches them. Nothing is saved; take a test as often as you like.

## Reading

```kanatest
title: Reading
desc: Pick or type the reading: a mix of both.
kind: read
level: voiced
count: 20
mode: mix
```
