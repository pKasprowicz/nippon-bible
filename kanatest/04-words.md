# 4. Real Words in Both Scripts

Everyday words from the hiragana and katakana courses, mixed. Every test mixes **hiragana and katakana**. There is no teaching here: each answer is marked at once, and at the end a report shows every character you met, coloured by how you did, with your weakest characters and a link to the unit that teaches them. Nothing is saved; take a test as often as you like.
