# 5. Look-alikes

The characters learners confuse most: within hiragana (ぬ and め, わ れ ね, さ and ち), within katakana (シ and ツ, ソ and ン, ク ケ タ) and across the two scripts (り and リ, か and カ, せ and セ). Every test mixes **hiragana and katakana**. There is no teaching here: each answer is marked at once, and at the end a report shows every character you met, coloured by how you did, with your weakest characters and a link to the unit that teaches them. Nothing is saved; take a test as often as you like.
