# 6. Final Exam

Everything at once: basic, voiced and combined characters of both scripts, read, typed and heard, with look-alikes and real words. Forty questions. Every test mixes **hiragana and katakana**. There is no teaching here: each answer is marked at once, and at the end a report shows every character you met, coloured by how you did, with your weakest characters and a link to the unit that teaches them. Nothing is saved; take a test as often as you like.
