# 3. Combinations, Small っ and the Long ー

Combined sounds such as きゃ and シュ, the extra katakana sounds for foreign words such as ファ and ティ, and, in words, the small っ/ッ that doubles a consonant and the katakana long mark ー. Every test mixes **hiragana and katakana**. There is no teaching here: each answer is marked at once, and at the end a report shows every character you met, coloured by how you did, with your weakest characters and a link to the unit that teaches them. Nothing is saved; take a test as often as you like.
