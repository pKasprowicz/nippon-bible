## Self-check

```quiz
? What does 中止 on a festival poster mean?
- Cancelled *
- Starts at noon
- Free entry
> Check before you travel.

? What is a mikoshi?
- A portable shrine *
- A food stall
- A dance troupe
> Chapter 9.

? What is the Japanese for cherry-blossom viewing?
- 花見 hanami *
- 花火 hanabi
- 月見 tsukimi
> 花火 is fireworks; 月見 is moon viewing.

? What is a furikae kyūjitsu?
- A substitute holiday after a holiday that falls on a Sunday *
- A sandwiched day off
- A shrine festival day
> Chapter 3.
```
