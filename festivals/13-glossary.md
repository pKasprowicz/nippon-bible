# Glossary

Japanese terms used in this handbook. The chapter where each is explained is given in the last column, and the explanation there carries the source.

## The calendar

| Term | Meaning | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| ***taiin taiyō reki*** 太陰太陽暦 | Lunisolar calendar: months by the Moon, kept in step with the Sun | 1 |
| ***kyūreki*** 旧暦 | The old calendar, used until 1872 | 1 |
| ***taiyōreki*** 太陽暦 | The solar (Gregorian) calendar, from 1873 | 1 |
| ***dai no tsuki*** / ***shō no tsuki*** 大の月・小の月 | Long (30-day) and short (29-day) months | 1 |
| ***urūzuki*** 閏月 | Intercalary month | 1 |
| ***daishō-reki*** 大小暦 | Picture calendar showing the long and short months | 1 |
| ***Ise-goyomi*** 伊勢暦 | Calendar printed at Ise, the most widely spread | 1 |
| ***tenmonkata*** 天文方 | The shogunate's official astronomer | 1 |
| ***eto*** 干支 | The sixty-year cycle of stems and branches; the animal of the year | 1 |
| ***kanreki*** 還暦 | 60th birthday, when the cycle comes round | 1 |
| ***[gengō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_era_name)*** 元号 | Era name, changed only when a new emperor succeeds | 1 |
| ***[nijūshi sekki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_term)*** 二十四節気 | The 24 solar terms | 2 |
| ***zassetsu*** 雑節 | Other seasonal days, such as *setsubun* and *higan* | 2 |
| ***doyō*** 土用 | The 18 days before each season begins | 2, 7 |
| ***hachijū-hachiya*** 八十八夜 | The 88th day from *risshun* | 2, 6 |
| ***nihyaku-tōka*** 二百十日 | The 210th day from *risshun*, said to bring storms | 2, 7 |
| ***[rokuyō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rokuy%C5%8D)*** 六曜 | The six-day cycle of lucky and unlucky days | 2 |
| ***wafū getsumei*** 和風月名 | Old Japanese names of the months | 2 |
