# Glossary

Japanese terms used in this handbook. The chapter where each is explained is given in the last column, and the explanation there carries the source.

## History and organisation

| Term | Meaning | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| ***Nippon Tetsudō*** 日本鉄道 | Nippon Railway, the first private railway (1881) | 1 |
| ***Tetsudō fusetsu-hō*** 鉄道敷設法 | Railway Construction Act (1892) | 1 |
| ***Tetsudō kokuyū-hō*** 鉄道国有法 | Railway Nationalization Act (1906) | 2 |
| ***Tetsudōshō*** 鉄道省 | Railway Ministry (1920) | 2 |
| ***[Kokutetsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_National_Railways)*** 国鉄 | Japanese National Railways, JNR (1949–1987) | 2, 4 |
| ***[JR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Railways_Group)*** | The six passenger companies and JR Freight that replaced JNR | 4 |
| ***daisan sekutā*** 第三セクター | "Third sector": a company in which local governments hold shares | 4, 10 |
| ***Eidan*** 営団 | Teito Rapid Transit Authority, Tokyo's subway (1941–2004) | 6 |
| ***sōgo chokutsū unten*** 相互直通運転 | Through running onto another company's tracks | 6 |
| ***geppu*** 月賦 | Monthly instalments, used by Hankyu to sell houses | 5 |
