# Lesson 5: Situations

Short dialogues for the situations you'll meet most often, plus the etiquette
that matters. **S** = staff / local, **You** = you.

## 1. Trains & subway

**Tips**
- Get an **IC card** (Suica, PASMO, ICOCA; also available as a phone wallet card).
  Tap in and out. It also works in konbini, lockers and vending machines.
  Top up = **chāji** (チャージ) at any ticket machine.
- Platform signs show **~番線** (platform number) and **~方面** (direction).
- If your card balance is too low at the exit, use the **精算 (seisan)** machine.
- Queue in the marked lines on the platform. Keep phone calls off, and talk quietly.
- Shinkansen: **指定席** reserved car vs **自由席** unreserved car.

**Useful lines**
- *Kono densha wa Shinjuku ni tomarimasu ka?* · Does this train stop at Shinjuku?
- *Shibuya wa nan-bansen desu ka?* · Which platform for Shibuya?
- *Doko de norikaemasu ka?* · Where do I change trains?
- *Shinkansen no kippu o kaitai desu.* · I'd like to buy a Shinkansen ticket.

```
You: Sumimasen. Kyōto made, shiteiseki o ichimai onegaishimasu.
     (Excuse me. One reserved seat to Kyoto, please.)
S:   Nan-ji no densha desu ka?   (Which time's train?)
You: Jū-ji goro, onegaishimasu.  (Around ten, please.)
S:   Jū-ji jū-go-fun hatsu desu. Ichi-man yon-sen en desu.
     (The 10:15 departure. ¥14,000.)
You: Kādo de daijōbu desu ka?
```

## 2. Taxi

- **空車** (kūsha, red light) in the windscreen = available.
- **The rear left door opens and closes automatically.** Don't touch it.
- Show the address on your phone map. Many drivers speak little English.

```
You: [Hoteru no namae] made onegaishimasu.  (To [hotel], please.)
S:   Hai.
You: Koko de ii desu.                         (Here is fine.)
You: Ryōshūsho, onegaishimasu.                (Receipt, please.)
```

## 3. Hotel / ryokan

```
You: Chekku-in, onegaishimasu. Yoyaku shite imasu. [Name] desu.
     (Check-in, please. I have a reservation. I'm [name].)
S:   Pasupōto o onegaishimasu.  (Your passport, please.)
You: Hai, dōzo.
```
- *Nimotsu o azukatte moraemasu ka?* · Could you keep my luggage?
- *Chōshoku wa nan-ji kara desu ka?* · What time is breakfast from?
- *Wi-Fi no pasuwādo wa?* · The Wi-Fi password?
- *Chekku-auto wa nan-ji desu ka?* · What time is check-out?

**Ryokan / tatami rules:** shoes off at the entrance. Use the slippers in the
corridor, take them off before stepping on tatami, and use the separate
**toilet slippers** only in the toilet. Wear the yukata **left side over right**.
Right over left is how the dead are dressed.

## 4. Restaurant

```
S:   Irasshaimase! Nan-mei sama desu ka?
You: Futari desu.                          (Two people.)
S:   Kochira e dōzo.                        (This way, please.)
You: Sumimasen! Eigo no menyū wa arimasu ka?
S:   Hai, dōzo.
You: Kore to kore o kudasai. Sorekara nama bīru o futatsu.
     (This and this, please. And two draught beers.)
     ...
You: Sumimasen, o-kaikei onegaishimasu.  (The bill, please.)
```

- Call staff by raising a hand and saying **sumimasen!** Many tables also have a call button (呼び出し).
- You'll get a wet towel (**oshibori**). Use it for your hands, not your face.
- Ramen and *teishoku* places often have a **ticket machine (食券)**: pay first, hand the ticket to staff.
- **Pay at the register by the door**, not at the table. Take the bill with you.
- **No tipping.** Leaving money behind confuses staff, and they may chase you to return it.
- Slurping noodles is fine. **Don't** stick chopsticks upright in rice or pass food chopstick-to-chopstick (both are funeral rituals).

**Dietary needs**
- *[X] arerugī ga arimasu.* · I have an [X] allergy.
  (ebi = prawn · kani = crab · kōnattsu = peanuts · komugi = wheat · tamago = egg · gyūnyū = milk)
- *[X] wa taberaremasen.* · I can't eat [X]. (niku = meat · buta-niku = pork · sakana = fish)
- *[X] nuki de onegaishimasu.* · Without [X], please.
- *Bejitarian desu.* · I'm vegetarian. Note that dashi (fish stock) is in almost
  everything, so vegetarian food is hard to find. Show a written card if it's important.

## 5. Konbini & shopping

```
S:   Irasshaimase.
S:   Fukuro wa goriyō desu ka?   (Do you need a bag?)
You: Daijōbu desu.               (No thanks.)
S:   Atatamemasu ka?             (Heat it up?)
You: Hai, onegaishimasu.
S:   O-shiharai wa?              (Payment?)
You: Kādo de.                    (By card.)
```

- Put money on the **small tray**. Don't hand it to the cashier.
- **Tax-free (免税)**: from November 2026 you pay the full price in the shop and
  get the consumption tax refunded at customs when you leave Japan. Show your
  **passport** at purchase, keep the goods unused, and ask: *Menzei dekimasu ka?*
- *Mite iru dake desu.* · I'm just looking.
- *Hoka no iro wa arimasu ka?* · Is there another colour?
- *Motto ōkii / chiisai saizu wa arimasu ka?* · A bigger / smaller size?
- **Bins are rare** in public. Carry your rubbish or use the konbini bins.

## 6. Asking for directions

```
You: Sumimasen. Eki wa doko desu ka?
S:   Massugu itte, hidari desu.     (Go straight, it's on the left.)
You: Chikai desu ka?                (Is it near?)
S:   Aruite go-fun gurai desu.      (About five minutes on foot.)
You: Arigatō gozaimasu!
```

Listen for **massugu** (straight), **migi** (right), **hidari** (left),
**shingō** (traffic light), **kado** (corner), **aruite** (on foot).
To make it easier, show your map: *Chizu de oshiete kudasai.* (Please show me on the map.)

## 7. Temples, shrines & onsen

- **Shrine (神社 jinja, torii gate)**: rinse your hands at the water basin. Then
  *bow twice, clap twice, bow once* at the offering box.
- **Temple (寺 tera)**: bow, no clapping. Some have incense to waft over you.
- Ask before photographing inside buildings: *Shashin o totte mo ii desu ka?*
- **Onsen**: wash and rinse thoroughly at the stools *before* entering the
  bath. Bathe naked. Keep your small towel **out of the water** (on your head is
  fine). Tattoos may be refused, so ask: *Tatū wa daijōbu desu ka?*

## 8. Emergencies & health

| Phone | |
|---|---|
| **110** | Police (*keisatsu*) |
| **119** | Ambulance (*kyūkyūsha*) & fire |
| 050-3816-2787 | Japan Visitor Hotline (JNTO): 24 h help in English |

| Japanese | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **tasukete!** | Help! |
| **kyūkyūsha o yonde kudasai!** | Call an ambulance! |
| **keisatsu o yonde kudasai!** | Call the police! |
| **byōin wa doko desu ka?** | Where is a hospital? |
| **guai ga warui desu** | I feel unwell |
| **koko ga itai desu** | It hurts here (point) |
| **[X] o nakushimashita** | I've lost my [X] (*saifu* wallet, *pasupōto*, *sumaho* smartphone) |
| **michi ni mayoimashita** | I'm lost |

Lost property often gets returned. Ask at the station office or the nearest
**kōban** (交番, police box).
