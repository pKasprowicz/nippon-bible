# Lesson 10: Cheat Sheet & Self-Test

## Cheat sheet (screenshot this)

**Sounds:** a i u e o like Italian · *r* = light tap · *u* in *desu/-masu* is
silent ("dess", "mass") · long vowels (ō, ū) last twice as long ·
double consonants = short pause (*kippu*) · keep every beat even, no stress.

**Scripts:** curvy = hiragana · angular + **ー** = katakana (loanwords, sound it out!) · complex = kanji (match the shape).

**Top 12 phrases**
| | |
|---|---|
| sumimasen | excuse me / sorry |
| arigatō gozaimasu | thank you |
| onegaishimasu | please |
| [X] o kudasai | [X], please |
| daijōbu desu | I'm fine / no thanks |
| [X] wa doko desu ka? | where is [X]? |
| ikura desu ka? | how much? |
| [X] wa arimasu ka? | do you have [X]? |
| kādo de daijōbu desu ka? | is card OK? |
| ~te mo ii desu ka? | may I ~? |
| wakarimasen | I don't understand |
| eigo, daijōbu desu ka? | is English OK? |

**Numbers:** ichi ni san yon go roku nana hachi kyū jū · hyaku 100 · sen 1,000 · **man 10,000**
**Things:** hitotsu futatsu mittsu · **People:** hitori futari san-nin

**Must-know kanji**
| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 入口 entrance | 出口 exit | 男 men | 女 women |
| 押 push | 引 pull | 開 open | 閉 close |
| 駅 station | 円 yen | 禁止 prohibited | 無料 free |
| 営業中 open | 準備中 closed | 税込 tax incl. | 現金 cash |
| 北 N | 南 S | 東 E | 西 W |

**Emergency:** 110 police · 119 ambulance · *tasukete!* = help!

**Subway:** match the line **colour + letter** and station code (G09) · 〜行 = bound for · choose your **exit number** before leaving the platform · short on fare? use the 精算機 · last trains around midnight.

**Food:** *-don* = rice bowl · *zaru* = cold noodles on a tray · *kake* = hot noodles in broth · 定食 = set meal · 大盛 = large · 時価 = market price, ask first · dashi (fish stock) hides in most savoury dishes · slurping noodles is fine.

**Never:** tip · make phone calls on trains · eat while walking · stand chopsticks in rice · wear shoes on tatami · enter the onsen unwashed.
**Always:** carry cash, a handkerchief and your passport · pay in **yen** on card terminals · queue · use both hands to give and receive.

## Self-test

Try every question before opening the answers.

### A. Pronunciation
1. How many beats does *Tōkyō* have?
2. What's the difference between *biru* and *bīru*?
3. How is *desu* usually pronounced?
4. Where does the stress go in *Hiroshima*?

### B. Recognition
5. Which script is this, and what does it say: **タクシー**?
6. Read: **メニュー**, **ビール**, **トイレ**
7. You're at the onsen. Which curtain is the women's bath: **男湯** or **女湯**?
8. A door says **引**. Push or pull?
9. A shop sign says **準備中**. Can you go in?
10. The menu says **¥1,200（税抜）**. Is that the final price?
11. How do you tell **シ** from **ツ**?
12. Write **3万円** in ordinary digits.

### C. Words & phrases
13. Say ¥3,000 and ¥10,000 in Japanese.
14. Ask "Where is the station?"
15. Order two of something you're pointing at.
16. The konbini cashier asks *Fukuro wa goriyō desu ka?* Politely say no.
17. Ask if you can take a photo.
18. Tell the taxi driver "To Kyoto Station, please."
19. What do you say before and after a meal?
20. Should you leave a tip in a restaurant?

### D. Getting around & customs
21. The platform display says **新宿行**. What does it tell you?
22. You're in Tokyo and switch from a Tokyo Metro line to a Toei line. Which gate do you use?
23. Your IC card balance is too low at the exit gate. What do you do?
24. In Osaka, are Umeda and JR Ōsaka station in different parts of the city?
25. You're visiting a Japanese home. Name two things to do at the entrance.
26. The card terminal asks whether to pay in EUR or JPY. Which do you choose?
27. An izakaya bill includes a small starter you didn't order. Is it a scam?
28. Name two of the three things to do before getting into an onsen bath.

### E. Food & dishes
29. What is the difference between *sushi* and *sashimi*?
30. You order *oyakodon*. What's in it?
31. The ramen shop asks how firm you want your noodles. Say "firm, please".
32. Is *kitsune udon* topped with fox meat?
33. Your skewer of *tsukune* arrives with a raw egg yolk. What is it for?
34. Which sake is more polished, *junmai ginjō* or *junmai daiginjō*?

<summary><strong>Answers</strong></summary>

1. **Four**: To-o-kyo-o.
2. *biru* = building, *bīru* = beer.
3. **"dess"**: the *u* is nearly silent.
4. **Nowhere**. Keep all four beats even: Hi-ro-shi-ma.
5. **Katakana**: *takushī* = taxi.
6. *menyū* (menu), *bīru* (beer), *toire* (toilet).
7. **女湯** (女 = woman).
8. **Pull** (押 = push).
9. **No**, it's closed (preparing).
10. **No**, tax is excluded. Expect about 10% more (8% for take-away food).
11. **シ** strokes sweep upward from the left; **ツ** strokes fall down from the top.
12. 3 × 10,000 = **¥30,000**.
13. *san-zen en*, *ichi-man en*.
14. *Eki wa doko desu ka?*
15. *Kore o futatsu kudasai.*
16. *Daijōbu desu.*
17. *Shashin o totte mo ii desu ka?*
18. *Kyōto eki made onegaishimasu.*
19. *Itadakimasu* (before) · *Gochisōsama deshita* (after).
20. **No.** Tipping isn't done in Japan.
21. The train is **bound for Shinjuku** (行 = *-iki*, destination).
22. The **transfer gate** (乗換改札, often marked in orange). Don't use a normal exit, or you'll pay twice.
23. Top it up at the **精算機** (fare adjustment machine) or go to the staffed window.
24. **No.** They're the same area under different names, linked underground.
25. Take off your shoes (pointing them to the door), say *o-jama shimasu*, hand over a small gift with both hands.
26. **JPY (yen).** The euro conversion rate is usually worse.
27. **No.** It's *otōshi*, a normal cover charge.
28. Undress fully (no swimwear), **wash before getting in**, keep your towel out of the water.
29. *Sushi* is the vinegared rice with its toppings; *sashimi* is sliced raw fish without rice.
30. **Chicken and egg** ("parent and child"), simmered with onion in sweet soy-dashi broth, on rice.
31. *Katame de onegaishimasu.*
32. **No.** It's topped with sweet fried tofu (*aburaage*), which foxes are said to love.
33. A **dip**: tsukune (chicken meatball) is dipped into the yolk.
34. **Junmai daiginjō**: at most 50% of the grain remains (ginjō: at most 60%).

**Score:** 27+ you're ready · 17–26 re-read the lessons where you missed answers · under 17 follow the study plan again.
