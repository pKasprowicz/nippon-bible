# Glossary

Japanese terms used in this handbook. The chapter where each is explained is given in the last column, and the explanation there carries the source.

## Manga and pictures

| Term | Meaning | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| ***[emakimono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emakimono)*** | Picture scroll, unrolled from right to left | 1 |
| ***gachūshi*** | Words written inside a picture next to the speaker; forerunner of the speech balloon | 1 |
| ***[gekiga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gekiga)*** | "Dramatic pictures": realistic, socially conscious manga from the rental-book era | 2 |
| ***[kashihon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kashihon)*** | Rental books; manga made for rental shops | 2 |
| ***[kibyōshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiby%C5%8Dshi)*** | "Yellow covers": satirical picture books for adults, Edo period | 1 |
| ***manga*** | Comics; for Hokusai, "sketches drawn as the fancy takes you" | 1, 2 |
| ***manpu*** | Conventional symbols on and around faces in manga | 4 |
| ***ponchi-e*** | "Punch pictures": Meiji comic picture books, named after *Japan Punch* | 1 |
| ***[shōjo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Djo_manga)*** / ***[shōnen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Dnen_manga)*** | Girl / boy; manga in magazines for each | 2 |
| ***[seinen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seinen_manga)*** / ***[josei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josei_manga)*** | Manga for young and adult men / for adult women | 2 |
| ***[tankōbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tank%C5%8Dbon)*** | Collected paperback volume (*komikkusu*) | 2 |
| ***toba-e*** | Edo-period books of comic drawings, named after Toba Sōjō | 1 |

## Speech

| Term | Meaning | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| ***ijin kotoba*** | Role language for "outsiders": foreigners, people of the past, non-humans | 4 |
| ***kyara gobi*** | Artificial "character endings" of aliens and robots | 4 |
| ***kyarakutā gengo*** | A character's distinctive speech that is not a shared stereotype | 4 |
| ***yakuwarigo*** | Role language: speech that calls up a stock character | 4, 14 |
