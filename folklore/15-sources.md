## Primary texts

Lafcadio Hearn (1850–1904) retold Japanese stories from books and from people he met. His books are quoted as a record of what he read and heard, not as a survey of Japanese belief.

- **Hearn 1894.** Hearn L. *Glimpses of Unfamiliar Japan* (first series). Boston and New York: Houghton Mifflin, 1894. Chapter 15, "Kitsune". [Project Gutenberg #8130](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/8130). *(Chapter 12)*
- **Hearn 1899.** Hearn L. *In Ghostly Japan*. Boston: Little, Brown, 1899. "A Passional Karma". [Project Gutenberg #8128](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/8128). *(Chapter 8)*
- **Hearn 1902.** Hearn L. *Kottō: Being Japanese Curios, with Sundry Cobwebs*. New York: Macmillan, 1902. Chapter "Gaki". [Project Gutenberg #55473](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/55473). *(Chapters 6, 9)*
- **Hearn 1904.** Hearn L. *Kwaidan: Stories and Studies of Strange Things*. Boston and New York: Houghton Mifflin, 1904. "The Story of Mimi-nashi-Hōichi". [Project Gutenberg #1210](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1210). *(Chapters 7, 9, 12)*
