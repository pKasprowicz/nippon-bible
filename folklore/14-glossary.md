# Glossary

Japanese terms used in this handbook. The chapter where each is explained is given in the last column, and the explanation there carries the source.

## Kami, shrines and ritual

| Term | Meaning | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| ***aramitama*** / ***nigimitama*** | The violent and the peaceful aspect of one [kami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kami) | 1, 7 |
| ***chinkon*** | Spirit pacification | 7 |
| ***[gongen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gongen)*** | "Provisional manifestation": a kami understood as a form of a buddha | 5 |
| ***harae*** (*harai*) | Purification | 3 |
| ***[hatsumōde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hatsum%C5%8Dde)*** | The New Year visit to a shrine | 1 |
| ***[honji suijaku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honji_suijaku)*** | "Original ground, manifested trace": buddhas appear as kami | 4 |
| ***jindō*** | Buddhist term in the *[Nihon shoki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nihon_Shoki)* for the realm of non-Buddhist deities; source of the word [Shinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinto) | 1 |
| ***jingi kanjō*** | Esoteric initiations into knowledge of the kami | 4 |
| ***[kagura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kagura)*** | Sacred dance and mime offered to the kami | 3 |
| ***kami*** | Deity; anything superhuman and extraordinary ([Norinaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motoori_Norinaga)) | 1 |
| ***katashiro*** | Paper substitute that carries a person's impurities away | 3 |
| ***[kegare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kegare)*** | Ritual pollution from death, blood and birth | 3 |
| ***kenmitsu*** | The exoteric–esoteric Buddhist system of medieval Japan | 1, 4 |
| ***[o-fuda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ofuda)*** / ***[o-mamori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omamori)*** | Shrine talisman / portable charm | 3 |
| ***[Ōharae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%8Charae_no_Kotoba)*** | The Great Purification prayer | 3 |
| ***[shinbutsu bunri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinbutsu_bunri)*** | The separation of kami and buddhas, from 1868 | 5 |
| ***[shinbutsu shūgō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinbutsu-sh%C5%ABg%C5%8D)*** | The combination of kami and buddhas | 4 |
| ***shinkoku*** | "Land of the kami" | 4 |
| ***shintai*** | The body or seat of a kami, such as [Mt. Miwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Miwa) | 1 |
| ***[shimenawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shimenawa)*** | Sacred straw rope marking protected space | 3 |
| ***shugendō*** / ***[yamabushi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamabushi)*** | Mountain asceticism / its practitioners | 4, 5 |
| ***taisai*** | A shrine's great annual festival | 3 |
| ***ujiko*** | Shrine parishioners | 1, 3 |
