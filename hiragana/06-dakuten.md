# Hiragana 6: ゛ and ゜ (Voiced Sounds)

Here's a shortcut: **25 "new" characters with nothing new to memorise**. Two small marks
change the sound of characters you already know:

- **゛ (two dashes, *dakuten*)** makes the consonant voiced: **k→g, s→z, t→d, h→b**
- **゜ (small circle, *handakuten*)** turns the **h** row into **p**

Learn one rule, and 25 sounds come for free.

**Your mission:** read **ありがとう** (thank you), **いりぐち** and **でぐち** (entrance
and exit), **てんぷら**, and three stations on Tokyo's Yamanote line.

## 2. Discover the rule

Before reading the table, work it out yourself. Discovering a rule yourself makes it
stick better than being told it (the generation effect).

```quiz
title: Work it out
? か is "ka". が is か with ゛. Say "ka" and "ga" with your hand on your throat. What changes?
- the vowel
- your throat vibrates for "ga" *
- nothing
> Voiced sounds (g, z, d, b) make your vocal cords vibrate. ゛ means "voice it".

? さ is "sa". So ざ (さ + ゛) is…
- za *
- ta
- da
> s → z, so ざ = za.

? は is "ha". With ゛ it's ば (ba). With ゜ it's ぱ. What's ぱ?
- fa
- pa *
- ma
> ゜ only appears on the は row and always makes "p": ぱ ぴ ぷ ぺ ぽ.

? し is "shi". What's じ (し + ゛)?
- zi
- ji *
- chi
> じ = ji (typing "zi" also works). It's the voiced version of "shi".
```

## 3. The whole table

| Base row | + ゛ | + ゜ |
|----------|------|------|
| か ka き ki く ku け ke こ ko | **が ga ぎ gi ぐ gu げ ge ご go** | |
| さ sa し shi す su せ se そ so | **ざ za じ ji ず zu ぜ ze ぞ zo** | |
| た ta ち chi つ tsu て te と to | **だ da ぢ ji づ zu で de ど do** | |
| は ha ひ hi ふ fu へ he ほ ho | **ば ba び bi ぶ bu べ be ぼ bo** | **ぱ pa ぴ pi ぷ pu ぺ pe ぽ po** |

**ぢ** and **づ** are rare: *ji* and *zu* are almost always spelled **じ** and **ず**.

## 5. Tell them apart

The hard part is not the shape, it's hearing and seeing the **small mark**.

```contrast
ば ぱ | Two dashes (゛) = b. Small circle (゜) = p.
び ぴ | Two dashes (゛) = b. Small circle (゜) = p.
か が | No mark = k. Two dashes = g.
た だ | No mark = t. Two dashes = d.
ふ ぶ | No mark = fu. Two dashes = bu.
```

## 10. Before you go

- In real life the marks are small. **Look closely at the top right** of every character.
- ありがとう has a long **ō** at the end (とう). Hold it: *a-ri-ga-to-o*. Unit 7 is all about these long sounds.
