# Hiragana 5: The や, ら and わ Rows, and ん

The last 11 basic characters. The や row has only **ya, yu, yo**, and the わ row only
**wa** and **を (o)**. **ん** is the only character that is a consonant on its own:
**n**, worth one full beat.

**Your mission:** read **すみません** (excuse me), **こんにちは** (hello), the onsen sign
**ゆ**, and the station **ひろしま**. After this unit you know **all 46 basic hiragana**.

## 2. Guess first

```quiz
title: Guess first
? ゆ is printed on the curtains of hot-spring baths. It's read "yu" and means…
- hot water *
- welcome
- towel
> ゆ = yu, hot water. Look for it on onsen curtains: 男湯 / 女湯 often have a big ゆ.

? る and ろ are almost identical. One has a loop at the end. Which one is "ru"? Think "a ruby ring".
- る *
- ろ
> る has the loop (a ring); ろ is an open road, no loop.
```

## 6. Tell them apart

This unit has the trickiest look-alikes. Give this drill your full attention.

```contrast
る ろ | る ends in a loop; ろ doesn't.
ね れ | ね curls into a loop; れ kicks out.
れ わ | わ's tail curls back inside; れ's tail kicks out.
ね わ | ね ends in a loop; わ's curve closes without one.
い り | り's right stroke is longer, and the two strokes join.
ゆ よ | ゆ is a U-shape with a line through it; よ is a crossbar with a looped foot.
ま よ | ま has two crossbars; よ has one.
ち ら | Both have a round belly. ち has a full crossbar on top; ら has only a small tick.
```

## 7. Three particles read differently

Three hiragana change their reading when they're used as **grammar particles**, small words that link a sentence together:

| Written | Normal reading | As a particle | Example |
|---------|---------------|---------------|---------|
| は | ha | **wa** | こんにちは · *konnichi**wa*** · hello |
| を | (o) | **o** | を is *only* used as a particle |
| へ | he | **e** | えきへ · *eki **e*** · to the station |
