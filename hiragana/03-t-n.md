# Hiragana 3: The た and な Rows

Two more families of five. Two sounds in the た row are irregular: **ち = chi** and
**つ = tsu** (the "ts" from *pizza*). Everything else follows the pattern.

**Your mission:** read **ちかてつ** (subway), **おとな** (adult, on ticket machines),
vending-machine **あたたかい** (warm), and the station **うえの** (Ueno).

## 2. Guess first

```quiz
title: Guess first
? に has a vertical stroke and two short lines. The two lines look like 二, the kanji for "two" (read "ni"). Which sound is に?
- na
- ni *
- no
> に = ni. A shape that looks like its meaning is easy to remember.

? つ is one big rolling wave. Which sound might it be?
- tsu *
- tu
- su
> つ = tsu, as in tsunami. Typing "tu" is also accepted in this course.
```

## 5. Tell them apart

```contrast
さ ち | Both have one crossbar. さ's bottom is a separate little curve bulging left, like a "c". ち's belly bulges right, like the number 5.
た な | た has two small strokes like こ on the right; な ends in a knot.
ぬ め | ぬ has an extra loop at the end; め doesn't. (め comes in Unit 4.)
ね れ | ね's tail curls into a loop; れ's tail kicks out. (れ comes in Unit 5.)
つ し | つ is a wave lying on its side; し is a hook standing up.
```
