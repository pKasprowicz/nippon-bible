# Hiragana 7: Combinations, Small っ and Long Vowels

Three small spelling tricks, and then you can read **any** hiragana word:

1. **Small ゃ ゅ ょ** merge with the character before: き + ゃ = **きゃ kya** (one beat)
2. **Small っ** = a short pause that doubles the next consonant: **きっぷ kippu** (ticket)
3. **Long vowels** are written with an extra vowel: **とうきょう Tōkyō**, **おばあさん obāsan**

These matter: they change meaning. **びよういん** (biyōin) is a hair salon; **びょういん**
(byōin) is a hospital.

**Your mission:** read **とうきょう**, **きょうと**, **きっぷ**, **しゃしん** (photo), **だいじょうぶ**
(it's OK), and ride the Yamanote line from Shibuya to Shinjuku.

## 2. Small ゃ ゅ ょ

Look at the **size**. Big や is "ya"; small ゃ attaches to the character before it.

| | + ゃ | + ゅ | + ょ |
|---|---|---|---|
| き | きゃ kya | きゅ kyu | きょ kyo |
| し | しゃ **sha** | しゅ **shu** | しょ **sho** |
| ち | ちゃ **cha** | ちゅ **chu** | ちょ **cho** |
| に | にゃ nya | にゅ nyu | にょ nyo |
| ひ | ひゃ hya | ひゅ hyu | ひょ hyo |
| み | みゃ mya | みゅ myu | みょ myo |
| り | りゃ rya | りゅ ryu | りょ ryo |
| ぎ | ぎゃ gya | ぎゅ gyu | ぎょ gyo |
| じ | じゃ **ja** | じゅ **ju** | じょ **jo** |
| び | びゃ bya | びゅ byu | びょ byo |
| ぴ | ぴゃ pya | ぴゅ pyu | ぴょ pyo |

The rule: drop the **i**, add **ya / yu / yo**. For し, ち and じ the *y* disappears: *sha*, *cha*, *ja*.

```quiz
title: Use the rule
? きょ is き (ki) + small ょ. How is it read?
- kiyo
- kyo *
- ko
> Drop the i of "ki", add "yo": kyo, one beat. "kiyo" would be written きよ, with a big よ.

? What's しゅ?
- shu *
- shiyu
- su
> し (shi) loses its i: sh + u = shu.

? What's the difference between きや and きゃ?
- None
- きや is two beats, ki-ya; きゃ is one beat, kya *
- きゃ is louder
> Size matters: a big や is its own syllable.
```

## 3. Small っ: the pause

Small **っ** isn't pronounced. It's a **one-beat silence** before the next consonant, which
is then written double in romaji. Clap it: *ki-(pause)-pu*.

| Without っ | With っ |
|------------|---------|
| きて · kite · come | きって · **kitte** · stamp |
| おと · oto · sound | おっと · **otto** · husband |
| かこ · kako · past | かっこ · **kakko** · brackets |

## 4. Long vowels

A long vowel lasts **two beats**. In hiragana it's written with an extra vowel:

| Sound | Written | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| aa | **ああ** | おばあさん · obāsan · grandmother |
| ii | **いい** | おじいさん · ojīsan · grandfather |
| uu | **うう** | くうこう · kūkō · airport |
| ee | **えい** (usually) or **ええ** | せんせい · sensei · teacher |
| oo | **おう** (usually) or **おお** | とうきょう · Tōkyō · おおさか · Ōsaka |

## 5. Hear the difference

These pairs differ only by a long vowel, a small っ or a small ょ. Your ear has to do the
work, so listen closely.
