# Hiragana Daily Review

Five minutes a day. Every character and word you've practised is scheduled here using
**spaced repetition**: the better you know something, the longer until you see it again.

## How the schedule works

Each item sits in one of six boxes. A correct answer moves it up one box, and a miss sends
it back to box 1.

| Box | Next review after | What you're asked |
|-----|-------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | 1 day | recognise it (read, or pick what you hear) |
| 2 | 2 days | recognise it or type it |
| 3 | 4 days | type the reading, or pick what you hear |
| 4 | 8 days | type the reading, or pick what you hear |
| 5 | 16 days | type the reading, or pick what you hear (counts as mastered) |
| 6 | 32 days | type the reading, or pick what you hear |

Why it works: each review comes just as you're starting to forget, and that effortful
recall is what makes memories last (the spacing effect and the testing effect).

**Tips**
- Review **before** you start a new unit, not after.
- Got many due after a break? Do 40 today and the rest tomorrow; the schedule copes.
- "Extra practice" drills your 15 weakest items without changing your schedule. Use it when you have spare minutes, like waiting for a train.
