# Hiragana 4: The は and ま Rows

Two families again. One irregular sound: **ふ = fu**, a soft "f" blown between the lips,
with no teeth (see [Lesson 1](../lessons/01-pronunciation.md)).

**Your mission:** read **はい** (yes), **つめたい** (cold, the blue label on vending
machines), **さしみ**, **みなみ** (south) and the station **はかた** (Hakata).

## 2. Guess first

```quiz
title: Guess first
? め looks like an eye, and in Japanese 目 (me) means "eye". What's the reading of め?
- mi
- me *
- mo
> め = me. The shape, the meaning and the sound line up.

? ほ looks like は with an extra bar on top. If は is "ha", ほ is probably…
- hi
- ho *
- he
> ほ = ho. Same family, so same consonant: h.
```

## 5. Tell them apart

```contrast
は ほ | ほ has an extra bar across the top; は doesn't.
け は | は has a loop at the bottom right; け doesn't.
ま も | ま has a loop at the bottom; も is a hook (like し) with two bars.
ぬ め | ぬ ends in a small loop; め doesn't.
め の | め is crossed by a second stroke; の is one single swirl.
へ く | へ is a hill (^); く is a beak opening left (<).
し も | も is し with two crossbars.
```
