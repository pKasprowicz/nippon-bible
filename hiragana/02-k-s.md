# Hiragana 2: The か and さ Rows

Ten new characters, in two families. Most hiragana are **consonant + vowel**, and the
vowels always come in the same order: **a, i, u, e, o**. So the か row is
**ka ki ku ke ko**, and the さ row is **sa shi su se so**. Note the one irregular sound:
し is **shi**, not "si".

**Your mission:** read **えき** (station), **すし**, **おいしい** (delicious) and the
station name **あさくさ** (Asakusa).

## 5. Tell them apart

The most common confusion in this unit is **き** vs **さ**. Count the crossbars.

```contrast
き さ | き has two crossbars (a kite); さ has one (a sword over a saddle).
こ い | こ is two horizontal strokes (coins on a table); い is two vertical strokes.
け は | け's right side is a simple crossed stroke. は (Unit 4) adds a loop at the bottom.
```

## 11. Before you go

- **Explain it to yourself:** why is it ka-ki-ku-ke-ko and not ka-ke-ki-ko-ku? (Because the vowel order is always a-i-u-e-o. Knowing the system halves the work.)
- **Spot it:** あさくさ, the station you just read, is the stop for Sensō-ji temple in Tokyo.
