# Hiragana 8: Real-World Reading and Final Test

You know every hiragana. This unit moves your skill from the screen to the street:
real signs, menus and station boards, where hiragana is mixed with kanji and katakana.

**Your mission:** pass the final test, then go hunting for hiragana on your trip.

## 2. Where you'll see hiragana in Japan

| Where | What you'll read | Example |
|-------|------------------|---------|
| **Station signs** | The station name, in big hiragana or next to the kanji | しぶや · 渋谷 |
| **Above kanji** (*furigana*) | Small hiragana printed over hard kanji, on maps, children's signs and some menus | 新宿 with しんじゅく above it |
| **Word endings** (*okurigana*) | Kanji carry the meaning, hiragana the grammar | 止**まれ** · *tomare* · STOP |
| **Vending machines** | Hot and cold labels | **つめたい** (cold) · **あたたかい** (warm) |
| **Onsen curtains** | Hot water | **ゆ** |
| **Shop and restaurant signs** | Friendly, traditional or handwritten names | **うどん** · **そば** · **おでん** · **だんご** |
| **Everyday phrases on signs** | Welcome, thank you | **ようこそ** (welcome) · **ありがとうございます** |

### Pick out the hiragana

In mixed text, hiragana are the round characters. Find them, read them, and you often
have the gist even without the kanji.

```quiz
title: Pick out the hiragana
? A sign says 押してください. Which part is hiragana, and how is it read?
- 押 · o
- してください · shite kudasai *
- 押して · oshite
> してください (shite kudasai) means "please do". The kanji 押 is "push": 押してください = please push.

? A road sign shows 止まれ. Read the hiragana.
- mare *
- tomare
- tama
> まれ (mare) is the grammar ending. With the kanji 止 (to) it reads tomare: STOP.

? A small sign next to a door reads おしはらい. What does it mean? Hint: お + しはらい (shiharai, payment).
- exit
- payment, pay here *
- push
> おしはらい (o-shiharai) means payment. You'll see it at tills and ticket machines, often as お支払い.
```

## 7. Keep it alive

Reading speed and long-term memory come from **use**, not from finishing a course.

- **Daily review**, 5 minutes, for the next month: [open it](09-review.md). After about a month most characters will be solid green.
- **Kana hunt in Japan:** every day of your trip, find and read **five** hiragana words in the wild: signs, packaging, station boards. Say them out loud.
- **Next step:** katakana, the angular script for foreign words like ホテル and コーヒー. See [Lesson 2](../lessons/02-writing.md) for the chart and the most useful loanwords.

🎌 *O-tsukaresama deshita!* Well done.
