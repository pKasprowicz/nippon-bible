# Glossary

Japanese terms used in this handbook. The chapter where each is explained is given in the last column, and the explanation there carries the source.

## Springs

| Term | Meaning | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| ***[onsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onsen)*** 温泉 | Hot spring; legally, spring water or gas at 25 °C or more, or with one of 19 substances | 1 |
| ***ryōyōsen*** 療養泉 | Therapeutic spring, defined by a stricter list | 1, 3 |
| ***sensitsu*** 泉質 | Spring type, such as chloride or sulphur spring | 3 |
| ***tanjun onsen*** 単純温泉 | Simple spring: at least 25 °C, under 1,000 mg of dissolved substances per kg | 3 |
| ***shinsō netsusui*** 深層熱水 | Deep hot water under plains, reached by deep wells | 2 |
| ***kasui / kaon / junkan*** 加水・加温・循環 | Water added / heated / recirculated: compulsory notices since 2005 | 3 |
| ***Yubatake*** 湯畑 | "Hot-water field" at the centre of Kusatsu | 7 |
| ***yumomi*** 湯もみ | Kusatsu custom shown with a song and dance at the Netsu-no-yu hall | 7 |

## Bathhouses

| Term | Meaning | Chapter |
|---|---|---|
| ***seyoku*** 施浴 | Charity bath offered by a temple | 4 |
| ***yokushitsu*** 浴室 | Bath hall, one of the seven principal halls of a Zen temple | 4 |
| ***furoya / yuya*** 風呂屋・湯屋 | Edo bathhouses: steam bath / tub bath | 5 |
| ***zakuroguchi*** 柘榴口 | Low board over the front of an Edo tub, keeping heat and steam in | 5 |
| ***irikomiyu*** 入込湯 | Mixed bathing, banned in Edo from the Kansei reforms | 5 |
| ***yuna*** 湯女 | "Bath women" of early Edo bathhouses, banned in 1657 | 5 |
| ***yuya-kabu*** 湯屋株 | Tradable right to run a bathhouse | 5 |
| ***[sentō](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sent%C5%8D)*** 銭湯 | Neighbourhood public bathhouse | 6 |
| ***kairyō-buro*** 改良風呂 | Meiji "improved bath", the model of the modern sentō (1877) | 6 |
| ***miya-zukuri*** 宮造り | Temple-like bathhouse architecture, mostly around Tokyo | 6 |
| ***bandai*** 番台 | Raised attendant's seat where fees are paid | 5, 6 |
| ***karan*** カラン | Tap at a washing place | 6 |

| ***sudomari*** 素泊まり | Room only | 8 |

| ***yukata*** 浴衣 | Light cotton robe | 8 |

| ***tōji*** 湯治 | A long stay at a spring, bathing to cure illness | 7 |
